Element Type Chart

Hitting a Pal with the element it's weak to deals bonus damage; the reverse is halved, so leading with the right element can swing a fight several-fold. Fire is the only element strong against two types; Neutral has no strength and only Dark beats it. Dual-type Pals can be hit through either element.

Breeding

Breeding Farm unlocks at level 19. Put a male + female in with a Cake and an egg appears — but the child isn't random.

The one rule: Combi Rank

Every Pal has a hidden combi rank (breeding power) — rare Pals are low, common chaff like Lamball runs to ~3,100. The game averages the two parents' ranks and hatches the Pal whose rank is closest. That's why hundreds of pairs can make the same Pal. Don't memorize combos — work backwards in a calculator. Fixed "unique combos" (Vanwyrm + Cinnamoth → Anubis) ignore the math entirely.

Passive inheritance

A child has up to 4 passive slots, pulled from the parents' combined pool plus some random rolls. Keep the parents' combined wanted passives at exactly four (a 2/2, 1/3 or 0/4 split) for the cleanest odds, and use clean 0-passive mates to isolate a trait. No duplicates. Legend only comes from Legendaries — breed it to transfer.

The five cakes

  • Cake — normal egg, no bonus
  • Mushroom Cake (tech 30) — better stats/potential
  • Veggie Cake (tech 47) — two eggs at once
  • Deluxe/Exquisite Veggie (tech 60) — mutation ~3%
  • Special Cake (tech 74) — boosts passive inheritance

Mutations (new)

~0.5–1% per egg (up to ~3% with Deluxe Veggie). The egg is purple with strange patterns, and the hatch comes with 90–100 IVs and at least two rainbow passives — sometimes a completely different, stronger Pal than the parents. Aegidron often hatches this way early.

Best Passive Traits

Traits roll randomly on catch, hatch or breed. Max 4 slots, no duplicates. Breed the ones you want onto your keepers.

⚔️ Combat

  1. Legend — +20% Atk / Def / Move (Legendaries only; breed to pass) S
  2. Immortality — +15% Atk, +100% regen, 5% lifesteal, no downside S
  3. Musclehead — +30% Atk, −50% Work (fighters only) A
  4. Ferocious — +20% Atk, no penalty A
  5. Element booster — e.g. Lord of the Sea, +20% element dmg A

Best stack: Legend + Musclehead + Ferocious + element booster (≈ +74% attack). Never put Musclehead on a base worker.

🛠️ Work

  1. Artisan — +50% Work Speed (grab first, cheap) S
  2. Lucky — boosts work + combat, no downside S
  3. Remarkable Craftsmanship — +75% Work Speed A
  4. Work Slave / Serious — work speed + endurance A
  5. Demon's Hand — +90% Work Speed A

Named stack: Artisan + Work Slave + Serious + Lucky (≈ +135% work speed). All-rounder pick: Lucky + Legend.

Best Food & the Meta

Food buffs don't stack — a new dish replaces the old one, and most last ~10 minutes and apply to Pals and you. So meal choice is a loadout: work food at base, attack food before a boss, defense food when you'll get hit.

  • Minestrone is the workhorse — +40% Work Speed and every ingredient (2× Tomato, 2× Carrot, 2× Onion, 1× Potato) grows in your fields. Mass-produce it at the Electric Kitchen.
  • Pizza is the endgame base food: the strongest combined Work Speed + Sanity recovery, so Pals stay fast and happy longer.

Fishing & Best Spots

Fishing (unlocks tech 15) catches Water Pals without spheres and pulls schematics, Pal Souls, and gold. Cast at a shadow and keep the fish in the green bar. Watch the sparkles: green = guaranteed Rainbow passive · purple = Alpha with a Gold passive · purple geyser = the Lunker passive. The Advanced Rod with the Depresso lure is best.

Overall Tips

  • Specialize your base. One Pal at Lv 8 in a single job beats one at Lv 3 across five — and it condenses far better. Lock each to a station with the Monitoring Stand so it never wanders.
  • Condensing is cheap now. Max rank (4★) takes 48 copies instead of 116. Each star raises one work suitability from the top down; 4★ raises them all by one. Keep spare copies of your specialists as fodder.
  • Capture bonus finishes in 5 catches (was 12), so grabbing 5–10 of a species for stats + condense fodder is fast. Sparkling fishing shadows and Alphas are the shortcut to Rainbow/Gold passives.
  • Breed, don't hunt, when you can. A fixed combo like Vanwyrm + Cinnamoth → Anubis arrives long before you could catch the Lv 47 desert Alpha.
  • Gear for the biome. Heat armor for Mount Obsidian (Axel) and the volcanic Feybreak pools; cold armor for the ice regions (Victor, Bjorn). The biome damages you before the boss does.
  • Towers gate the story. Clearing all 8 faction towers gives the Key Spheres that open the World Tree finale. Each also drops Ancient Tech Points and a fast-travel point.
  • Puffolt is a base multiplier — its partner skill gives +1 Generating Electricity to every Pal at the base. Pair it with an electric-powered production line.
  • Feed good food + build the work-speed structures (up to +20%). Combined with Artisan/Work Slave passives, base output compounds hard.
